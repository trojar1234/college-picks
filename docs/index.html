Home team in bold; scores are listed away–home, matching the matchup. Model picks shows the model's side for every game against the current line (the closing line once a game is final). Most picks are close calls; only games marked BET are flagged edges: the model and line differ by at least the threshold (7 points on spreads and totals) and nothing makes the projection shaky. Amber flags are edges with a reason for caution: low confidence, a QB change, or wind/rain the model doesn't account for. "Check news" marks games where the model and market differ by 8+ points; in past seasons the market was closer in about two of three of those. The backtest shows the model's edge is against early-week lines, so a bet is only worth acting on early, and only if the line hasn't already moved to the model's number. Lines are never used as model inputs.
