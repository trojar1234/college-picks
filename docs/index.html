Model picks shows the model's side in every game against the current line (the closing line once a game is final). Most picks are close calls; only BET games clear the 7-point bar with nothing making the projection shaky. A FLAG clears the bar but comes with a caution: a QB change, wind or rain (the model doesn't use weather), or a gap of 8+ points ("check news"), where in past seasons the market was right about two times in three. A bet keeps the line it was first flagged at. The model's edge is against early-week lines, so a bet is worth acting on early and only if the line hasn't already moved to the model's number. Lines are never used as model inputs.
