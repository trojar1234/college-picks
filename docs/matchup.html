Matchup

How to read this page
  • Pick any two teams and where they'd play. The projection uses the same ratings and formula as the weekly predictions, as of the latest update. Unlike the weekly projections, it doesn't include QB-change or manual adjustments, and it isn't a betting pick.
  • Spread is from the favorite's side (−12.5 means favored by 12.5). Win chance and the 80% range come from how far off past predictions have been (a typical miss of about 16 points on the margin), so even a 10-point favorite loses about a quarter of the time.
  • Fair odds are the moneyline that matches the win chance with no sportsbook margin. A book's price longer than this on the same side would be value if the model is right.
  • The what-if slider shifts Team 1's strength by a number of points; the presets use typical values (a backup QB is worth about 7 points, a key non-QB starter about 3). It's a scenario, not a projection, and the card shows the unadjusted number next to it.
  • ± next to a rating is its 80% range: how far the true strength could plausibly be from the estimate, wider early in the season and for teams with few games.
  • Win chance and the range use the same method as the weekly projections: a normal curve around the projected margin with the size of past out-of-sample misses (it was better calibrated than the score simulation). The weekly numbers can still differ slightly when a manual adjustment (overrides.csv) applies; QB changes are flagged, not adjusted.
  • Share a matchup by copying the page link: it remembers the teams and the venue.
