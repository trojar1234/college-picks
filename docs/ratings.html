How to read this page
  • Rating is how many points better than an average FBS team each team would be on a neutral field, using the same ratings and formula as the game projections. The gap between two teams' ratings is roughly the neutral-field spread; the Matchup page gives the exact number.
  • Move is the change since last week: places up or down in the ranking, then the rating change in points.
  • Record is the record so far. Expected wins adds each remaining scheduled game's win chance to the wins so far, so 10.4 means the model expects about 10–11 wins (bowls and title games count once they're scheduled). The team panel also shows how hard the remaining schedule is.
  • Conferences compares conferences by their teams' average rating. Schedule left is the average rating of the opponents still to play, adjusted for where the games are.
  • ± is the rating's 80% range: how far the true strength could plausibly be from the estimate. It shrinks as a team plays more games.
  • Offense / Defense are opponent-adjusted expected points (EPA) per play with garbage time removed, compared with an average FBS team. The small grey number is the rank. On defense, lower is better.
  • Tap a column header to sort, and tap a team for its results, rating over the season, upcoming games with projections, and a link to compare it with any opponent.
